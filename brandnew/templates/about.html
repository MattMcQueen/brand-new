About Brand New

Brand New lists the books and albums that came out in the last week (or the last two weeks, if a genre had a quiet week) and those due over the next three months, by genre.

The lists are updated automatically once a day, usually in the morning (UK time). It's run by Matt, who writes the Matt Rarely Writes blog. What this site earns helps keep the blog free of ads.

What this site does, and what it doesn't

Brand New is for finding out what's new and what's coming soon, and for giving you a quick way to look for it at different shops. That's all it tries to do.

  • It isn't a price comparison site

    It doesn't show prices, and it can't tell you where something is cheapest.

  • It can't tell you who sells it or has it in stock

    The site doesn't check any shop, so it doesn't know whether a shop sells a particular book or album, or has it in stock. Every link is a starting point, not a promise.

  • Every link opens a search

    Every shop link, Amazon included, opens a search on the shop's own site: for the title and author of a book, or the artist and title of an album. Those searches may find other editions, or nothing at all.

    The links don't go straight to a book's page because the ISBN in the public data is often the ebook's, which a shop may not list, and the site doesn't check. A search finds whichever editions the shop sells.

  • Not every book comes in every format

    Many books never come out as ebooks or audiobooks, or they arrive later, and the site can't tell which.

  • Dates change, and the lists aren't complete

    Release dates move, especially for books, and the lists are built from public data that misses some releases. Check the date with the shop before you order.

How the Amazon links work

{{ config.AMAZON_DISCLOSURE }}

The "on Amazon UK" buttons, and the smaller "Kindle" and "Audible" buttons under them, are affiliate links. An affiliate link is an ordinary link to a shop with a code added to it, so the shop knows which website sent you. Mine is {{ config.AMAZON_TAG }}, and you'll see it at the end of the Amazon address as tag={{ config.AMAZON_TAG }}.

  • How I'm paid

    If you follow one of those links and then buy something, Amazon pays me a small commission, a percentage of the price, as thanks for sending you. It generally counts anything you buy within 24 hours of clicking, not only the item you clicked on.

  • You don't pay a penny more

    The price is exactly the same as if you'd gone to Amazon yourself: the commission comes out of Amazon's share, not your pocket. Amazon doesn't tell me who you are; I only see totals, such as which items were ordered through the site.

  • You never have to use them

    If you'd rather, search Amazon yourself, or buy from your local bookshop or record shop. The "More on Google Books" links are not affiliate links, and nobody pays to appear on this site.

Where the data comes from

  • Albums

    The ListenBrainz fresh releases feed, ranked by how many ListenBrainz listeners each artist has, with genres from MusicBrainz. MusicBrainz and ListenBrainz data is released under CC0 by the MetaBrainz Foundation.

  • Album covers

    The Cover Art Archive.

  • Books and book covers

    The Google Books API.

Privacy

  • This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or adverts.
  • If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is remembered in your own browser and never sent anywhere.
  • Covers are loaded from the Cover Art Archive and Google, so, as with any website, those services can see your IP address.
  • Once you click through to Amazon, Google Books or another shop, that site's own privacy policy applies.
  • The "Support me" button loads Ko-fi's donation form only when you click it; from then on, Ko-fi's privacy policy applies to that form.
